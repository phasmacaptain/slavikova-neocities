I live with nine cats, which means my house is basically a very soft, very chaotic little kingdom.

Salem, Nhoque and Nina were here first. Then Penélope arrived as a rescued pregnant mama and gave us five more: Joaquim, Daenerys, Frodo, Daemon and Eminem. The five kittens were born at home, so I got to watch the whole tiny dynasty happen in real time.

Some of them are shy, some are loud, some need a little extra care, and all of them have very strong opinions. This page is their permanent corner of Slavikova.

cat_notes (ฅ^•ﻌ•^ฅ)_□×

Penélope’s arrival changed the whole family tree: she was rescued while pregnant, and Joaquim, Daenerys, Frodo, Daemon and Eminem were all born at home. Watching them grow up together has been equal parts adorable and absolute chaos.

Salem is the senior gentleman, Nhoque is a tiny black cat force of nature, Nina is the shy survivor of the group, and Penélope is absurdly sweet. The younger five are now their own little generation of household troublemakers.

Living with nine cats is less like having pets and more like managing a tiny municipality: litter boxes, feeding logistics, vacuuming, fur on everything, territorial negotiations and an unreasonable amount of love.

I want this page to become a proper archive of their photos, quirks, favorite sleeping spots and the stories that are too good to disappear into a social feed. If you have a personal site with a pet page, please sign the guestbook. I love finding other tiny internet animal museums!!
